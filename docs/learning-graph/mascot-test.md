# Mascot Style Guide

This page previews all seven Sage admonition styles. Use it to verify mascot images render correctly after adding pose images to `docs/img/mascot/`.

!!! mascot-neutral "A Note from Sage"
    <img src="../../img/mascot/neutral.png" class="mascot-admonition-img" alt="Sage neutral pose">
    This is the **neutral** style — used for general sidebars, introductions, or any content that doesn't call for a specific emotional tone.

!!! mascot-welcome "Welcome!"
    <img src="../../img/mascot/welcome.png" class="mascot-admonition-img" alt="Sage waving welcome">
    This is the **welcome** style — used at the start of every chapter. Sage introduces what's coming and sets the stage for learning.

!!! mascot-thinking "Key Insight"
    <img src="../../img/mascot/thinking.png" class="mascot-admonition-img" alt="Sage thinking">
    This is the **thinking** style — used for key concepts and important connections. This is where Sage points out something worth pausing on.

!!! mascot-tip "Sage's Tip"
    <img src="../../img/mascot/tip.png" class="mascot-admonition-img" alt="Sage giving a tip">
    This is the **tip** style — used for practical hints and helpful guidance. Sage has aggregated wisdom from many neighborhoods.

!!! mascot-warning "Watch Out!"
    <img src="../../img/mascot/warning.png" class="mascot-admonition-img" alt="Sage warning">
    This is the **warning** style — used for common mistakes and pitfalls. Sage has seen this error before and wants to save you the trouble.

!!! mascot-encourage "You Can Do This!"
    <img src="../../img/mascot/encouraging.png" class="mascot-admonition-img" alt="Sage encouraging">
    This is the **encouraging** style — used for difficult content where students may feel stuck. Even complex aggregation functions clicked eventually!

!!! mascot-celebration "Well Done!"
    <img src="../../img/mascot/celebration.png" class="mascot-admonition-img" alt="Sage celebrating">
    This is the **celebration** style — used at the end of chapters and major milestones. Every node in your knowledge graph just got a stronger signal!
