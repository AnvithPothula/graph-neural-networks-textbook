# CS224W Fall 2021 Schedule

**Note:** This is the offering corresponding to the YouTube playlist (60 videos).
**Instructor:** Jure Leskovec
**Course archive:** http://snap.stanford.edu/class/cs224w-2021/
**YouTube playlist:** https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLoROMvodv4rPLKxIpqhjhPgdQy7imNkDn

The 2021 offering has a slightly different topic selection than 2025 — it includes PageRank, Label Propagation, community detection, and subgraph mining, which the 2025 version replaces with newer topics (Relational Deep Learning, LLMs+Graphs, Agents+Graphs, Foundation Models). Both versions are valuable source material for the textbook.
